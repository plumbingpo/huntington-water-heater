Restaurant Services

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. When the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and staging temporary hot water where possible.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.
